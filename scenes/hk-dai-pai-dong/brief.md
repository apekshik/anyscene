# Hong Kong dai pai dong, late night after rain

**Place.** A licensed dai pai dong (open-air cooked-food stall) on the pavement of a narrow old street in a Hong Kong
tenement district (Sham Shui Po / Sheung Wan feel). A battered green steel kitchen cart with roaring wok burners
against a tenement wall, a corrugated-tarp awning on steel poles, folding round tables and red plastic stools on
the pavement and spilling into the road. The street runs downhill toward the harbour; the far skyline is a soft
backdrop only.

**Time / weather.** About 1:30 AM. It has just rained hard; now a light drizzle. Everything wet, puddles in the road.

**Mood words.** steamy, neon-soaked, worn, warm-in-the-cold, alive-after-hours.

**Lens style.** Anamorphic-feel 2.39:1, 24–40 mm for the walks, 85–135 mm for the wok/teapot details. Messy handheld
walk takes, long and uncut.

- **Stall side** (left looking down the street): tenement facade plane at **x = −4.0**, pavement x −4 … 0 at z = 0,
  kerb face at x = 0 (15 cm drop), road x 0 … 7.4 at z = −0.15, far pavement x 7.4 … 9.6 at z = 0, far facades at x = 9.6.
- **Stall footprint:** x −4.0 … −1.6, y −0.5 … 6.0. Kitchen cart + wok range along the facade (front faces +X, into the street).
- **Awning:** corrugated tarp from the facade at z 3.1 sloping to z 2.55 at x = −0.2, y −1 … 7; steel poles at x −0.3.
- **Seating:** 4 round tables + 1 square table with 16–20 stools on the pavement (x −1.5 … 0) and in the road (x 0.4 … 3.2).
- **Walk-in side:** the road, y < −2. The handheld walk starts out in the road and walks toward the woks (−X).

## Zones
1. Wok station (cart, wok range with two burners, flames, steam, utensils rail, sauce shelf above).
2. Prep/back shelves along the wall (bowls, pots, rice cooker, tubs, jars), crates and gas cylinders underneath.
3. Seating under and beyond the awning (tables, stools, teapots, dishes, toilet-roll napkins, newspapers).
4. Street: kerb, guard railing, water barrier, parked car and scooter, street lamp, drains, puddles.
5. Facades: shuttered shops at ground level, neon/lightbox signs stacked above, AC units, windows, laundry poles.

## Practicals
- 5–6 bare tungsten bulbs on cords under the awning (2700 K), one enamel-shaded bulb over the wok.
- Two wok-burner flames (blue core, orange lick when tossing) — emissive volumes/meshes + small orange point lights.
- A fluorescent tube over the back shelves (cool white, slightly green).
- Neon/lightbox signs on the facades (red, cyan, pink, amber) — fictional names only.
- Sodium/LED street lamp over the road, shuttered-shop security lights, a few lit tenement windows.
- Sky: low cloud lit orange-magenta from the city. Wet ground gives every light a long reflection.
