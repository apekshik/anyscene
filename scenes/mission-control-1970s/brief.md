# Mission control, 1970s — brief

**Brief.** A 1970s space mission control room at night during a long shift. Tiered rows of beige and grey consoles with
glowing green CRTs, switches and lit buttons; a big wall display with an orbital ground track and telemetry; binders,
printout spools, coffee cups, overflowing ashtrays and cigarette haze; fluorescent ceiling panels partly off; windows onto
a night launch pad. No people.

**Fiction.** The agency is the *Aurora Flight Directorate* (AFD). The mission is **PERSEUS 7**, an orbital flight on
revolution 112. Ground stations are birds: KESTREL, OSPREY, HARRIER, MERLIN. Console positions use plain job words
(TRAJECTORY, GUIDANCE, TELEMETRY, COMMS, LIFE SUPPORT, POWER, PROPULSION, RECOVERY, NETWORK, FLIGHT DIRECTOR,
ORBITAL ANALYSIS). No real agency names, logos, patches or brands anywhere; every label is invented and set in real
font text objects or generated screen textures.

**Time and weather.** About 03:40, dry, clear night. The launch pad 3 km away is floodlit (cold xenon + sodium).

**Mood.** Tired, smoky, humming, institutional, quietly tense.

**Look.** Concept `refs/mission-control-1970s/concept_wide_0.png` (approved). Green phosphor everywhere, warm desk-lamp
pools, a cold cyan-green wall display, most overhead troffers off. Anamorphic 2.39:1, 35 mm-ish lenses, film grain.

## Hero spots

1. Row A centre console (ORBITAL ANALYSIS): ashtray, binders, coffee, the CRT and the wall display behind.
2. The wall display seen down the console rows from row C (flight director's view).
3. The right-wall printer / teleprinter corner with the paper spool spilling.

## Practicals

- every CRT (green P1 phosphor, a few amber), console lamp buttons and event-light strips;
- the wall display (cyan-green map + amber/green text) — the biggest light in the room;
- 4–5 gooseneck/arm desk lamps (warm 2700 K);
- 3 of 18 ceiling troffers on (cool white, one flickering-looking dim);
- the launch-pad floodlight spill through the windows (cold, low);
- an exit sign over the door (red), the coffee maker's warmer light.

Haze: a uniform low-density volume in the room (cigarette smoke), denser near the ceiling.
